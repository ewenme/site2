The masterful use of FMV was worth the (heavily discounted) price of entry, alone. Combat is solid and weighty, big upgrade over the originals repetitive encounters. The meandering, unfocused plot definitely lost me a bit towards the end and the *Twin Peaks* love-in felt a little overwrought, but the vibes and atmosphere is all there. Saga's mind palace has a nice pay off but it got really tiresome in her sections (which edged out Alan Wake's imo). Could've been a masterpiece, ended up a mostly fun jaunt with some sublime touches.
