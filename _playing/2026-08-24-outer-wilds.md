Immediately obvious why this is such a treasured game for lots of people. One of one in presentation, player freedom and environmental storytelling. I was just a little bit too dumb to make it through without checking reddit for hints a few times and outright solutions to puzzles once or twice which took the edge off for me. The family friendly vibes and art design turned me off initially (I actually put this down for a long time 'til recently), but my advice is to persevere and things will click pretty fast. The 22-minute loop also makes it a great pace for decrepit, washed gamers like me who get tired from clicking the buttons for very long.
